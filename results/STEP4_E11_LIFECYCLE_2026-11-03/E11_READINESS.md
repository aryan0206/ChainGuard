# E11 lifecycle readiness — 3 November 2026

**Verdict:** Existing registry and verifier code can support focused **engineering checks** for most A4/M5 lifecycle categories. A frozen, independently witnessed E11 research campaign is **not ready**. A3T/A4T implementation and parity are absent; the existing tests use ephemeral generated fixtures and do not retain exact per-case raw inputs/outputs as a research ledger. See [matrix](E11_LIFECYCLE_MATRIX.md), [execution](E11_EXECUTION_RESULTS.md), [evidence audit](E11_EVIDENCE_AUDIT.md), and [final status](E11_FINAL_STATUS.md).

The frozen [protocol](../../docs/EVALUATION_PROTOCOL.md), [experiment matrix](../../docs/EXPERIMENT_MATRIX.md) and [Architecture Lock §19](../../docs/ARCHITECTURE_LOCK.md) require independent expected run/challenge, exact singleton and multi-session inventory, pinned signer/configuration, repeatable nonconsuming inspection, complete fresh acceptance after confirmed durable commit, missing/extra/wrong/stale/duplicate rejection, restart and concurrent submission behavior, failed/ambiguous commit handling, and expectation preservation after failed submissions. A4 wraps A3 with durable acceptance; A4T requires A3T. Acceptance authenticates a complete submitted run under expected context; it does **not** certify benign behavior or prevent replay of tool execution.

Existing [current-source M6Q evidence](../completion_sprint_2026-11-02/FIVE_HOUR_SPRINT/live_m6_current/fresh_submission.json) records one preregistered singleton `ACCEPTED` with `COMMITTED` durability. The [readback](../completion_sprint_2026-11-02/FIVE_HOUR_SPRINT/live_m6_current/fresh_submission_readback.json) records reopened `ACCEPTED` and duplicate `REJECTED/ALREADY_ACCEPTED`. These are bounded engineering observations, not a full E11 matrix. The selected tests in [execution](E11_EXECUTION_RESULTS.md) use fresh temporary `fixture.sqlite`/`registry.sqlite` files, never the prohibited `audit.sqlite` or `acceptance_registry.sqlite` paths. They do not create a research-qualified E11 population.

**Execution gate:** A4T/A3T support, predeclared case IDs and exact expected outcomes, independently frozen assessor inventory/key/configuration, raw case-level inputs/outputs and registry boundary witnesses, full failure ledger, and an approved denominator/exclusion plan remain missing. No frozen protocol or expected result was changed to fit a test. The two blocked variant categories in the [matrix](E11_LIFECYCLE_MATRIX.md) cannot be executed without new implementation and approved cases.
